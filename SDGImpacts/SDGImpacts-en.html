Sustainable Development Goals Impact Themes

A mirror of the official UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) Taxonomy.

This vocabulary uses the UN's official IRIs as the primary identifiers for each goal. It retains the original commonapproach.org IRIs, which are now marked as deprecated, for backward compatibility.

Abstract

This Sustainable Development Goals Impact Themes codelist is based on the UN Sustainable Development Goals and republished by Common Approach to Impact Measurement as an RDF codelist. The primary identifiers for goals are the official UN IRIs (e.g., http://metadata.un.org/sdg/1). Legacy IRIs (e.g., https://codelist.commonapproach.org/codeLists/SDGImpacts#SDG1) are retained for backward compatibility but are deprecated.
